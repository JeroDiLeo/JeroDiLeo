## 🌟 Featured projects

### 📚 apuntesHUB: marketplace for study notes, with AI

A full-stack educational marketplace built for Software Engineering 1, now in beta. Students buy and sell university notes, organized by university, subject and professor.

- **Marketplace:** free preview of the first pages, built-in PDF reader (no forced downloads) and payments with Mercado Pago.
- **AI study tools:** chat with your notes with citations to the exact pages, a study mode with practice questions, and search by topic.
- **Classes:** a section where teachers can charge for classes, with content viewable inside the platform and no download option.
- **Security:** designed to prevent leaks of the material, with per-user session limits and other content protection measures.
- **My focus:** backend and security, including the database, authentication and authorization, content protection and the AI/RAG pipeline for PDFs.

**Tech:** TypeScript, Next.js, Supabase (PostgreSQL), Vercel, Mercado Pago, GitHub Actions

🌐 Site: https://www.apunteshub.com/
🔗 Repo: https://github.com/nicorfalcon/apuntesHUB
